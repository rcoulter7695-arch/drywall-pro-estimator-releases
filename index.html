See it work

Built around how you actually hang board

Blueprint takeoff

Open a PDF or image plan, set the scale for each page, and outline rooms. Wall and ceiling areas add up for you, with openings, stairways and extra areas.

Sheet counts by wall height

8 ft and tall walls figure on 4×12 sheets. 9 ft walls figure on 54″×12′ sheets so there's no strip to fill at the bottom. Compare 8 to 16 ft board lengths.

Mud by the box

Joint compound is counted in boxes, not buckets, for ProForm Lite Blue and All Purpose, along with screws, tape and primer.

Totals by floor or section

Tag rooms by floor or section and see a separate breakdown for each one.

Save jobs and print

Save every takeoff as a job file to reopen later. Print estimates or save them as PDF with your company name and logo, and attach up to 12 job photos.

English or Spanish

Switch the app to Spanish for your crew. It runs fully offline, and only connects when you click Check for updates.

Install

Windows

  1. On the release page, open Assets and download the .exe file.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC," click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Drywall Pro Estimator from the Start menu.

Linux

  1. On the release page, open Assets and download the .AppImage file.
  2. Make it runnable and start it (use your file's name): 
    chmod +x Drywall*.AppImage
./Drywall*.AppImage
  3. On Arch, if it won't start, install FUSE: sudo pacman -S fuse2
